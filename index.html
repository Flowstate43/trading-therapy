About

    Tip of the day

    Write the plan first

    If you can’t say the trade in one sentence — why you’re in, where you’re wrong, and how much you can lose — you’re not ready to click.

    Glossary term

    Support

    A price area where buying has historically stepped in. It’s a reference point, not a promise that the market will bounce.

    Mindset note

    Missing a move is allowed

    Forcing a trade just to feel involved is usually more expensive than sitting out. There will be another setup.
